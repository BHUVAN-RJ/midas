Hydration Sidekick: five directions

Each column shows the Sidekick's main screen and its small and medium Home Screen widgets. Drag "Today" to see each one fill. Tap a preset on any phone or widget to log. Tags under each column say whether its widget can be made with the widget pieces the app has now, or needs a new app build.

What each direction needs

DirectionMain screenWidget, with today's piecesWidget, at its best
A. StillPage onlyRing stands in for the glassNew "fill" piece: a shape that fills to a value
B. GrowPage onlyLeaf symbol + ring; no growing plantNew "stage" piece: picks a picture by value (seed, sprout, plant, bloom)
C. MistPage onlyRing + text + buttons: works as drawnSame
D. GlowPage onlyRing + text + buttons: works as drawn, glow is flatGlow on the ring (a setting on the ring piece)
E. TidePage onlyBar stands in for the sea lineNew "fill" piece (same as A)

Every direction needs two shell additions for the widget to be right at all, whatever the look: the day's total must start again at midnight without the Sidekick being opened, and the widget must show the amount in L or oz. Both are in the plan (docs/briefs/hydration-sidekick.md).
