Care Reminders · icons

8 options for the care-reminders manifest icon, at the sizes the shell draws. 1 to 6 fit the manifest as it is; 7 and 8 need a shell change. Glyphs are web approximations of the SF Symbols named.

12345678

Where the shell draws a Sidekick icon

  • Home grid, 60 pt, 4 columns, name under it in caption size (SidekickHomeGrid.swift).
  • Sidekicks widget (small, medium, large: 2×2, 4×2, 4×4), 44 pt, name at 11 pt (FolderWidget.swift). In tinted and clear modes iOS maps the tile to the accent colour by luminance.
  • Both use SidekickIconView (Shared/SidekickIcon.swift): corner 22.5% of the side, glyph at 45% of the side, semibold, always white, hairline edge black 8%.
  • Not drawn anywhere else: the Reminders widget header is the word “Today”; the widget picker lists names only; no Spotlight, Controls or Shortcuts; notifications show the Midas app icon.
  • Reminders does not use its manifest icon (checklist on #FF9500): the shell draws its W2 artwork by Sidekick id.

What the manifest accepts

  • "icon": { "symbol": "…", "background": "#RRGGBB" }. Nothing else: no glyph colour, no second colour, no artwork.
  • The deploy check (backend/scripts/bundle-tools.mjs) requires a non-empty symbol and a 6-digit hex. It does not check the symbol exists.
  • A name iOS does not know draws a blank tile. The shell targets iOS 17.0, so a symbol must be from 2023 or earlier in Apple's table.
  • With the glyph fixed at white, 5 of the 14 list colours give white the higher contrast: Slate, Cocoa, Charcoal, Teal, Forest. Options 1 to 6 use those.
