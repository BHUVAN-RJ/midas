jess: listening colour and icon · round 3

1 · Listening colour

Saturation drives arousal, not hue

Muted colours read near grey and calm. Every rejected colour was saturated. Valdez & Mehrabian 1994; Wilms & Oberfeld 2018

"Blue = trust" is not proven

Only "blue beats red" holds, with small effects and no replication. Kosova 2025 review; Elliot 2015

Motion proves listening

People miss colour-only states (37% read speaker lights right). Movement or a shape raises it. Nest, Sonos: white that pulses. Kunchay 2021; Portnoff 2015

2 · jess icon: pick the "s"

Caveat "j", "J" and "e", as in the Midas icon. Only the "s" changes. Centred, Midas size.
