Add Widget demo, aimed at one widget

Midas shows three widgets in the iOS gallery, in this order: Sidekicks (the folder), Reminders, Sidekick; three sizes each, so nine pages. The demo swipes past the others, stops on the one this sheet is for, dims the rest, and rings its name and preview. The same sheet works for any target: switch it below.

Steps it plays

  1. Touch and hold the Home Screen
  2. Tap Edit, then Add Widget
  3. Tap Midas (the row is ringed, the rest dim)
  4. Swipe to Sidekick: the pages slide past the others, then the target's name and preview are ringed and everything else dims
  5. Tap Add Widget

Shell note

The gallery order is the bundle order (MidasWidgetsBundle.swift: Folder, Reminders, Sidekick). Sidekick is last, 7th of 9 pages. Moving it first would make it the first page after tapping Midas, and the swipe step would go away for Sidekick. That is a one-line change in the bundle; not made.
