What Shortcuts can show

✓ Apple's symbols

Shortcuts tile, Spotlight and Action button take an Apple symbol only (AppShortcut systemImageName; no image option in iOS 18, 26, 27).

✕ Our own drawing

Shows blank in Shortcuts (Apple forum 758159, 795525). Works only in Control Center / Lock Screen controls.

Tile colour

We pick 1 of Apple's 15 (shortcutTileColor). No black or white. Colours below are close, not exact.

✕ Off limits

siri, apple.intelligence, message: Apple-only symbols.

Does “Ask jess” look like replacing Siri?

✓ Others ship it

ChatGPT: “Open ChatGPT Voice” on Action button (Dec 2025). Gemini: 6 controls incl. Talk Live (Mar 2025). Claude: “Open Claude”. Perplexity: voice assistant on Action button (Apr 2025).

✓ No rejections found

None for 2024–26. No rule against an “Ask <name>” label.

✕ Apple's line

HIG: “Never impersonate Siri … or provide a response that appears to come from Apple.” Guideline 5.2.5: nothing “confusingly similar” to an Apple product.

✕ 2012

Apps rejected as “too similar to Siri”. One passed after dropping a round mic icon that looked like Siri's.

Side button

Users may swap Siri for another voice app on the side button: Japan only, iOS 26.2+. EU: not confirmed.

People

No study found on people confusing other assistants with Siri.
