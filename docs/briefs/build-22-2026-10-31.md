## Founder decisions

**Words**
- The word "Repair" is now **"Edit"** everywhere a person sees it (repair layer, Make, cards,
  notifications, jess). Code names may stay `repair`.
- After Send in the Edit layer: the Volt tag says **"On it"**, then **"Editing"** (A2).
- Hint, the first 5 sends only, then never: **"Close anytime. Edits finish on their own."** Comes
  out of the Edit pill like a speech cloud (shape **F3 "Frosted bubble"**, founder 2026-10-31; was F1),
  sits under the pill, appears right after Send while the person is still on the screen, fades after
  3 s.
- When an edit could not be made: the person sees **"Check back in some time."** and the founder is
  alerted (push to the founder plus the job in the founder's Make inbox with what was asked, why it
  failed and the job link). The founder fixes it by hand and publishes.
- Make's sender name is **"Make"**.
- Make first open, once: C1 sheet, **"Built by Hand"** / **"I build your first version myself, so it
  arrives near perfect."** / button **"Continue"**, signed **"RJ"** in the hand.

**jess and repair voice**
- Edit layer: when the keyboard comes up, voice stops; words already heard stay, typing continues
  after them. Tapping the orb listens again (keyboard goes down).
- jess listening ends after **2.5 s** of silence after the first word (was 1.2 s), maximum **30 s**
  (was 15 s).

**Cards**
- Swipe sideways to delete: Workdesk cards (remove from the log) and home cards.
- Home shows only **Not Done** cards; they stay until swiped away. Done, Working, Sent to Edit and
  Waiting for Connection cards never show on home (Workdesk only). An edit that later turns Not Done
  brings its card to home.

**Settings and delete** (`settings-delete-2026-10-31.html`)
- Way in: **E1**, the person's initial in a small circle, top right of the home grid.
- Settings: **S2** pushed page, plain list (name, Sidekicks, Sign Out, Delete Account), but **no
  back arrow**: swipe from the left edge goes home, as everywhere in Midas.
- Delete a Sidekick: **press and hold on the home tile, exactly like removing an app on iOS**
  (hold → tiles jiggle with a minus badge; tap the minus). Then the **Midas sheet with the tile
  (C3 on the page)**: "Delete Water?" / "Its data goes too." / red "Delete" pill / "Cancel". No
  "Edit" option in the menu; it is delete only. **jess and Make cannot be deleted** (no badge).
  Every other Sidekick can.
- Delete Account: **A1** alert "Delete Account?" / "All Sidekicks and data go too." / "Delete
  Account" / "Cancel"; then **X1** its own screen "Your account will be deleted within 7 days." /
  "OK", then the signed-out welcome. Server records the request and alerts the founder; the founder
  carries out the deletion (only the founder deletes data).

**Water and Reminders**
- Water widget at 0 L: **option A** (part 1 of the Water mockup): a `floor` in
  `SidekickWidgetFiller.filledFill`, the page sends it.
- Water remove (Amogh asked, so build and ship): in his copy, removing a drink is easy to find and
  works for earlier days too.
- Reminders: the Alert choice (Normal / Time Sensitive / Alarm) also in the toolbar while typing a new
  reminder. Apple Reminders "Urgent" imports as Alarm, if EventKit exposes the flag (check; if not,
  say so). An hourly time ticked early counts.

**Icons**
- Every Sidekick tile has a light and a dark variant (today the Reminders tile stays light in dark
  mode). The repair agent and Make's builder always write both when they build or change a
  Sidekick.

**New accounts**
- New accounts get jess, Make and the founder's everyday Sidekicks (the founder builds them now);
  wire the sign-up assignment once they exist.
