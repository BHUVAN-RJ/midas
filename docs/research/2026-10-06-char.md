# Research note: Char (YC Summer 2025), 2026-10-06

A research record only. Nothing here changes the design doc or any decision. It records what
was looked up and what was found, for the agent versus app line in `## Bets and Falsifiers,
2026-10-06` of the design doc.

## What was asked

The founder described "a notepad that does things": a notepad where every task written down
is extracted by an LLM and worked on until done, and asked for it to be identified and written
up. The company is **Char**, by Fastrepl, Inc.

## Facts about Char

- Batch: YC Summer 2025. Founded 2023 as Hyprnote. Three to four people. Registered in Seoul,
  South Korea on the YC profile; listed as San Francisco elsewhere.
- Founders: John Jeong (CEO, "designing interfaces for humans and agents"), Deokhaeng Lee (CS at
  Duke), Yujong Lee (open source).
- Funding: 500 thousand dollars seed, January 2025, YC. No later round found.
- Pitch, verbatim from the YC profile: "The AI notepad that knows and works for you." "Type a
  checkbox, and an agent picks it up, researching, drafting, or scheduling on your behalf."
  Landing page: "Apple notes on steroids" and "Writing it down should start the work, not
  another inbox."
- How it works: the user writes notes and tasks in plain language. Checkboxes turn into agent
  actions. The agent asks clarifying questions, drafts emails and spreadsheets, gathers context
  from connected work apps (Slack, Gmail, Figma, Linear, Google Docs and on-screen content are
  named across sources), books meetings and travel, and keeps a wiki of what it did. The user
  approves before anything is sent or booked.
- Status: closed private beta behind a signup form. No pricing published. Notes stored
  on-device by default. No public API, MCP server or OAuth integrations detected.
- History: the company's first product was Hyprnote, an open-source, on-device meeting
  notetaker (Product Hunt launch August 11, 2025, 79 upvotes). On May 3, 2026 it was renamed
  Anarlog, kept open source and free, and Char became the new product. Stated reason: the two
  products "were trying to live under one brand. They couldn't." Users of the notetaker were
  confused by delegation features and users who wanted automation asked if they had to take
  meeting notes first.
- No user, retention or revenue numbers are public.

## How it relates to Midas

1. **It is the founder's agent lane, done well.** Char is the case the founder named as "where
   there is a need for agents, we definitely need to add agents." The tasks it takes on
   (research, drafting, scheduling, booking) are the unbounded or one-off kind where the Bets
   section already says an agent wins. It does not compete with a tracker, a list or a reminder,
   which is the Sidekick shape. Char is evidence for the line drawn in the Bets section, not
   against it.

2. **The interface is the point, not the agent.** The product's claim is that writing a note is
   enough to start work, with no form, no prompt box and no chat. That is the same bar as the
   design doc's "remind me at 3pm tomorrow" baseline: natural language in, the thing done, no
   agent on display. Char treats the notepad as the app and the agent as plumbing behind a
   checkbox. Same shape as Midas treating the generator as the only agent.

3. **A pivot caused by mixing lanes.** Char's own account is that a finished, focused product
   (meeting notes) and an open-ended delegation agent could not share one product. That is a
   small data point for keeping agents out of Sidekicks unless a specific app needs one, and
   for shipping a per-app specialized agent as its own thing later rather than threading an
   agent through every app.

4. **Work context, not personal.** Every named connector is a work tool. Char is pointed at
   knowledge workers and their inboxes, which is the same drift Koomen's RFS makes from personal
   to colleagues. It does not touch the phone, notifications, sharing with named friends or
   personal data, so it is not a competitor to v1.

5. **Watch item.** If Char or something like it adds a "make me a small tool" checkbox that
   produces a persistent artifact, it crosses into the generator. Nothing found suggests that.
